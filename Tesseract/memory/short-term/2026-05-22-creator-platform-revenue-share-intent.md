# Creator Platform Revenue Share Intent

## Source

User clarified on 2026-05-22 that the creator platform should not stay flat-rate for creators who materially profit from TWB-derived commercial use.

## Durable Direction

- Keep the low tier accessible.
- If someone makes significant money from the TWB creator platform, Bob wants a small percentage to return to him/the project.
- Preferred planning direction should investigate revenue-share / commercial-license language rather than informal profit-share language.

## Reasoning

- Profit is easy to distort through expenses and accounting structure.
- Gross revenue attributable to TWB-derived commercial work is easier to define.
- A threshold can protect hobbyists and small creators while still capturing upside from major commercial success.
