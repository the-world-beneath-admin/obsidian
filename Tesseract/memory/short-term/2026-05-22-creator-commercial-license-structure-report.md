# Creator Commercial License Structure Report

## Scope

Shared platform / creator-platform commercial licensing structure for The World Beneath.

## What Changed

- Created an internal creator commercial license structure.
- Created a current task brief for the commercial licensing gate.
- Updated shared-platform overview, roadmap, index, and open questions.

## Draft Structure

- Tier 0: community / noncommercial participation.
- Tier 1: `$20/month` Creator Access for small creators and early experiments.
- Tier 2: Commercial Creator License after `$100,000` in TWB-attributable covered gross revenue over a trailing 12-month period.
- Starting royalty candidate: `2%` of covered gross revenue above `$100,000`, paid quarterly.
- High-success review: custom commercial license review after `$1,000,000` in covered gross revenue over a trailing 12-month period.

## Research Used

- Unreal Engine licensing uses gross revenue directly attributable to a product and a royalty threshold model.
- Creative Commons NonCommercial definitions are flexible and not enough by themselves for TWB commercial creator enforcement.
- Kickstarter does not allow offering backers equity, financial returns, or loans, so creator-platform rewards must avoid investment/revenue-share framing.
- Stripe Tax / Connect docs emphasize deciding who handles tax/reporting obligations before platform/marketplace payment implementation.
