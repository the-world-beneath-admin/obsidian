- Draft quality depends on the locally running model and Ollama availability.
- Long prompts or slow local inference can make drafting feel delayed; the Electron bridge currently waits up to 120 seconds.
- The scanner still uses lightweight public HTML/JSON parsing, so source layout changes may reduce candidate quality.
