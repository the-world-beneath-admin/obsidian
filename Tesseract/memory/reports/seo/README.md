# SEO Reports

SEO reports are working evidence, not permanent strategy.

Specialists write reports here after bounded keyword, competitor, Steam tag, store-page, creator, content, or campaign research. The orchestrator reviews reports and decides what becomes permanent memory.

## Naming

Use:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD-short-research-topic.md
```

Example:

```text
2026-05-11-occult-farming-keywords.md
```

## Required Format

```markdown
# SEO Report - YYYY-MM-DD - Short Research Topic

## Task

What was requested.

## Research Question

The exact question being answered.

## Result

What was found or produced.

## Sources Reviewed

- Source, date checked, and reason used.

## Signals

- Evidence from search, competitors, Steam pages, campaigns, players, or tests.

## Hypotheses

- Testable ideas suggested by the research.

## Decision Candidates

- Recommendations needing orchestrator or user approval.

## Risks

- What might be misleading, weak, overbroad, or unsupported.

## Memory-Worthy Notes

- Items that may deserve promotion after review.

## Do Not Promote To Memory

- Drafts, weak guesses, rejected ideas, or unsupported claims.

## Follow-Up Recommendations

- Next research or tests.
```

## Promotion Rule

Do not treat a report as strategy. Promote only reviewed, stable items into the wiki using Fact, Signal, Hypothesis, Decision, Warning, Open Question, or Superseded labels.
