## Memory-Worthy Notes

- Decision - Steam Coming Soon should be treated as the main-game wishlist funnel for The World Beneath.
- Decision - The first two World Keys should release free on itch.io before they are used as Kickstarter proof.
- Decision - Kickstarter should be the funding and campaign layer around playable proof, not the free-game hosting platform.
- Warning - Do not make Glassroot Garden sound like the parent game.
- Warning - Do not promise account sync, unlocks, carryover, societies, trade, or territory pressure unless the specific public build supports those claims.
- Open Question - Which two World Keys are the first public free releases?
- Open Question - Is Glassroot Garden officially the first World Key release?
