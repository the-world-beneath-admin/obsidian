## Website Hero Draft

Headline:

> Run dungeons. Grow creatures. Shape the world beneath.

Subhead:

> The World Beneath is a shared-world RPG where dungeon runs feed creature growth, crafting, inventory progression, and a larger hidden world shaped by player societies.

Body:

> Venture below the ordinary world into a hidden RPG ecosystem built around dungeon runs, creature growth, crafting choices, and long-run progression. Follow development, join the community, and help shape what comes next.

CTA direction:

> Primary: Join the community.

> Secondary: Follow development.

Risk:

- If the target page shows no gameplay visuals, the headline may need a supporting line that explains dungeon runs and creature growth faster.

## Future Store Short Description Draft

Short description:

> The World Beneath is a shared-world RPG where dungeon runs build your creatures, unlock crafting paths, expand your inventory, and push you toward a hidden world shaped by player societies.

Capsule hook:

> Dungeon runs, creature growth, crafting, and a hidden shared world.

Risk:

- Store copy must not imply live society, trade, or territory systems unless the store build supports them.

## Creator / Press One-Liner Draft

One-liner:

> The World Beneath is a shared-world RPG where dungeon runs feed creature growth, crafting, inventory progression, and a larger hidden world shaped by player societies.

Short creator pitch:

> It is built around a readable RPG loop first: run dungeons, grow creatures, craft stronger paths forward, and follow the world as it expands into societies, trade, territory pressure, and community-driven systems.

Risk:

- For creator outreach, avoid making society/territory systems sound playable unless the pitch is tied to a future roadmap or development update.
