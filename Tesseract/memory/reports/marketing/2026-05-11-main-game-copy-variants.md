## Copy Variants

### Variant 1 - Balanced Loop First

Headline:

> Run dungeons. Grow creatures. Shape the world beneath.

Short pitch:

> The World Beneath is a shared-world RPG where dungeon runs feed creature growth, crafting, inventory progression, and a larger hidden world shaped by player societies.

Longer paragraph:

> Venture below the ordinary world into dungeon runs that build your creatures, unlock crafting paths, expand your inventory, and push you deeper into a hidden RPG ecosystem. The World Beneath is built around a concrete progression loop first, then widens into societies, trade, territory pressure, and community-driven world systems as the project grows.

CTA direction:

> Follow development, join the community, and help shape what comes next.

Best use:

- Website hero.
- Future store short description.
- Press/creator one-liner foundation.

Risks:

- "Shape the world" must stay framed as future/shared-world direction unless the target build supports live society or territory systems.
- The headline may still be slightly broad unless paired with screenshots or concrete loop visuals.

### Variant 2 - Creature Growth First

Headline:

> Grow strange creatures through every dungeon run.

Short pitch:

> A creature-growth dungeon RPG where runs, rewards, crafting choices, and inventory decisions push your team deeper into a hidden shared world.

Longer paragraph:

> Build your path through dungeon encounters, creature growth, crafting materials, and progression choices. The World Beneath uses dungeon rewards and long-run systems to make each creature and inventory decision part of a larger world designed for societies, trade, territory pressure, and connected account identity.

CTA direction:

> Follow the creature and dungeon systems as they expand.

Best use:

- Store-page A/B test.
- Trailer description focused on creatures.
- Creator outreach to creature collector / party RPG audiences.

Risks:

- Could overpromise creature collection if the first public build does not visibly support enough creature identity.
- Slightly weaker at explaining the shared-world differentiator.

### Variant 3 - Hidden World First

Headline:

> A hidden RPG world is taking shape beneath the ordinary one.

Short pitch:

> Run dungeons, grow creatures, craft stronger paths forward, and join the community forming the shared world of The World Beneath.

Longer paragraph:

> The World Beneath starts with the RPG loop: dungeon runs, creature growth, crafting, inventory progression, and deeper challenges. Around that loop, the project is building toward a larger shared world with societies, trade, territory pressure, World Keys, lore gateways, and a community that can follow the world as it forms.

CTA direction:

> Join the community and follow the world as it grows.

Best use:

- Community/account conversion page.
- Devlog intro.
- Lore gateway pages where the game still needs to remain visible.

Risks:

- Softer as a store hook.
- Could drift toward lore/community-first messaging if separated from concrete gameplay visuals.
- Needs the first sentence or surrounding UI to show dungeons and creatures quickly.

## Recommended Variant

Approved by user on 2026-05-11: use **Variant 1 - Balanced Loop First** as the current main-game copy foundation.

Reason:

- It follows the approved positioning rule most cleanly.
- It names the concrete loop before the shared-world promise.
- It can serve website hero, store short description, press one-liner, and creator pitch foundations without becoming too narrow.

Recommended review copy:

> Run dungeons. Grow creatures. Shape the world beneath.

> The World Beneath is a shared-world RPG where dungeon runs feed creature growth, crafting, inventory progression, and a larger hidden world shaped by player societies.

## Risks

- Risk - Any copy mentioning societies, trade, territory pressure, or shaping the world must not imply those systems are fully live unless the target build supports them.
- Risk - "Shared-world RPG" remains somewhat abstract and should be paired with concrete verbs, screenshots, or short loop explanations.
- Risk - "Creature-growth dungeon RPG" may perform better with cold players, but that needs testing rather than assumption.
- Risk - Community/lore CTAs should not replace game-loop clarity.

## Memory-Worthy Notes

- Decision - Variant 1 is approved as the current main-game copy foundation.
- Warning - Public copy should keep "dungeon runs, creature growth, crafting, inventory progression" near any "shared-world RPG" language.
- Open Question - Should the first website hero use the direct command headline or the fuller one-liner?

## Do Not Promote To Memory

- Do not treat any variant as final public copy yet.
- Do not promote these variants as validated conversion winners.
- Do not use Variant 2 as primary unless the user explicitly chooses creature-growth-first positioning.
- Do not imply societies, trade, or territory systems are live in a public build without checking the build target.
