# Stretch Goals

## Stretch Goal Strategy

Stretch goals should be exciting, but they cannot blow up the project.

For The World Beneath, the cleanest stretch goals are:

- small web-based **World Key minigames**,
- digital books/novellas,
- audiobook upgrades,
- more canon archive depth,
- more Creator Kit assets,
- and better AI coordinator features.

Do not make huge new games into stretch goals. Keep the stretch goals small, shippable, and connected to the world.

## What A World Key Is

A **World Key** is a small, focused web minigame or interactive lore tool that reveals one system inside The World Beneath.

Each one should do three jobs:

1. Give players something small and fun to interact with.
2. Teach part of the world framework.
3. Add usable canon structure for future creators.

The Garden game is the proof-of-world entry point before Kickstarter.

After that, World Keys can become stretch goals.

## World Key Ideas

### World Key: Alchemy

A small web minigame where players combine materials, intent, pressure, and corruption risk.

Possible systems:

- common material + hidden catalyst,
- pure intent vs corrupted intent,
- failed mixtures producing filth residue,
- recipe discovery,
- creator hooks for potions, bombs, ritual oils, toxins, and strange medicines.

### World Key: Mining

A mining/resource minigame where players dig into natural and unnatural veins.

Possible systems:

- iron, silver, salt, bone, black glass, old stone,
- unstable seams,
- buried ruins,
- alien metal,
- cursed ore,
- World War I trench connections for buried artifacts and battlefield filth.

### World Key: Timber and Foraging

A wood harvesting and natural-resource minigame.

Possible systems:

- old-growth timber,
- grove spirits,
- cursed roots,
- ash wood,
- ritual herbs,
- fungi,
- sacred and profane materials.

### World Key: Enchanting

A minigame about putting intent into objects.

Possible systems:

- anchor object,
- emotional charge,
- ritual shape,
- failure state,
- corruption leakage,
- temporary enchantment vs permanent mark.

### World Key: Salvage and Relics

A search-and-identify minigame for ruins, trenches, shipwrecks, abandoned labs, and hidden battlefields.

Possible systems:

- relic fragments,
- faction stamps,
- hidden maps,
- dangerous artifacts,
- cursed heirlooms,
- alien debris.

### World Key: Bestiary

A creature identification minigame.

Possible systems:

- tracks,
- remains,
- witness statements,
- ritual signs,
- filth traces,
- false myths vs true history.

### World Key: Rituals and Intent

A ritual-building minigame.

Possible systems:

- symbol placement,
- intent alignment,
- sacrifice/cost,
- contamination risk,
- faction-specific ritual styles,
- accidental summoning.

### World Key: Cartography

A hidden-map minigame.

Possible systems:

- real-world location overlays,
- ley lines,
- sealed sites,
- ancient roads,
- trench lines,
- underground networks,
- secret organization routes.

## Important Guardrail

Do not promise a huge book unless the money and timeline support it.

Use careful wording:

> **Book stretch goals may be delivered as novels, novellas, or lore books depending on final scope. The goal is to expand the canon without delaying the core Phase One delivery.**

This lets you fulfill the promise without trapping yourself.
