# Kickstarter Campaign Pitch Framework

## Campaign Title

Best title:

# The World Beneath: A Game That Seeds a Living Creator-Driven Universe

Alternate titles:

- The World Beneath: A Video Game, Creator Kit, and Living Story World
- The World Beneath: Help Launch a Community-Built IP
- The World Beneath: A Free Web Game, Trenchworks, and a Creator-Grown Universe

## Opening Hook

Use this as the top of the Kickstarter page:

> **This is a video game first. But the game is only the seed.**

> **The World Beneath is a hidden-world video game universe where magic, aliens, secret organizations, fairy tales, monsters, and old conspiracies are all real. The Garden web app will be free before this Kickstarter launches. This campaign funds the next step: Trenchworks, the Creator Kit, the living canon archive, the AI canon coordinator, and the community system that lets creators and fans grow the world together.**

## The Clean Pitch Order

Do not start by explaining the whole business model. Start with the game and the world.

### 1. The Game

I am making a video game universe called **The World Beneath**.

The first public playable piece is the free Garden web app. The first paid game/code reward is Trenchworks, a World War I trench warfare game with The World Beneath twist.

### 2. The World

The World Beneath is our world, except the hidden layer is true.

Magic is real. Aliens are real. Secret organizations are real. Fairy tales are buried history. Intent has power. Filth can manifest. The things people whisper about are part of the canon.

### 3. The Bigger Concept

The game seeds a living IP.

I have written a book and an audiobook. I am building the first games and the canon framework. But the long-term goal is to let creators build under the IP in many formats.

### 4. The Creator System

Creators should be able to make books, comics, 3D prints, games, visual novels, mobile games, lore projects, videos, and other things inside the world.

The Creator Kit gives them a starting point.

### 5. The Canon System

The living canon archive tracks what is true in the world.

The AI coordinator helps creators check their ideas against canon and receive suggestions so the universe can grow without becoming chaos.

### 6. The Community Loop

Creators need people to create for.

Fans need a world worth gathering around.

The World Beneath puts both sides in one place.

### 7. The Kickstarter Ask

This Kickstarter funds Phase One: Trenchworks, the Creator Kit, the living canon archive, the AI coordinator prototype, the community hub, legal setup, and one year of founder runway.

## Kickstarter Video Script

### Opening

I am making a video game universe called **The World Beneath**.

It is set in our world, but with one major difference: all the hidden things are real.

Magic is real. Aliens are real. Secret organizations are real. Fairy tales are not fairy tales. They are history.

### The First Playable Proof

Before this Kickstarter launches, the Garden web app will be released for free.

That is the first public proof of the world.

This campaign funds the next phase: Trenchworks, the Creator Kit, the living canon archive, the AI coordinator, and the community framework that lets this grow beyond one person.

### Trenchworks

Trenchworks is the first paid game/code reward.

It is a World War I trench warfare game with The World Beneath twist: intent has power, filth can manifest, impossible things hide beneath real history, and the war is not only what the history books said it was.

### The Bigger Idea

This is not just a game.

The game is the seed for a living IP.

I have also written a book and an audiobook to expand the story. But I do not want this world locked behind one studio forever.

I want creators to be able to build inside it.

Books. Comics. 3D prints. Small games. Visual novels. Mobile games. Videos. Lore projects. Things I have not even imagined yet.

### How It Stays Organized

To make that possible, I am building three creator tools.

First, the Creator Kit.

Second, the living canon archive.

Third, the AI canon coordinator.

The Creator Kit gives creators the starting rules and assets.

The canon archive tracks what is true in the world.

The AI coordinator helps creators check ideas against the world and get suggestions so the IP can grow without collapsing into chaos.

### Why It Matters

AI is making it easier for people to create, but it does not automatically give them a community.

Creators still need people to create for.

Fans still need worlds worth caring about.

The World Beneath is meant to bring both sides together.

Community through story.

A rising tide lifts all ships.

### The Ask

The Kickstarter goal is $150,000.

That funds Phase One: one year of runway, Trenchworks delivery, the Creator Kit, the living canon archive, the AI coordinator prototype, legal setup for creator participation, and the first community system.

I am not asking you to just back a game.

I am asking you to help seed a world.

## Kickstarter Page Section Order

1. **Hero Section** - title, trailer, one-line pitch.
2. **This Is A Game First** - explain Garden and Trenchworks.
3. **The World Beneath** - hidden-world premise.
4. **What Makes This Different** - creator-grown IP model.
5. **The Creator Kit** - how creators participate.
6. **Living Canon Archive** - how the world stays coherent.
7. **AI Canon Coordinator** - how creators get guidance.
8. **Community Through Story** - creator/fan loop.
9. **What The Money Funds** - $150,000 budget.
10. **Rewards** - simple tier chart.
11. **Stretch Goals** - World Keys and books.
12. **Risks and Challenges** - honest but confident.
13. **Closing Ask** - help seed the world.
