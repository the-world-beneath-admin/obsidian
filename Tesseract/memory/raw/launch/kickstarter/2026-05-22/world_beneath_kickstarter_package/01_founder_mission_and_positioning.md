# Founder Mission and Project Positioning

## Founder Mission Statement

**My mission is to create a video game that seeds a living IP, then open that world so creators and fans can build it into something bigger than I could ever make alone.**

This starts as my game. I am creating the first playable experience, the first story foundation, the first book, the first audiobook, the first canon framework, and the first version of the world.

But the goal is not to build a closed IP where one studio controls everything and everyone else only gets to watch.

The goal is to create a new model for a video game universe:

- I seed the world.
- I establish the first canon framework.
- I build the first games and story foundation.
- The Creator Kit gives people a real way to create under the IP.
- The living canon archive keeps the world organized.
- The AI coordinator helps creators check their ideas against the world.
- The community grows the universe together.

This is not fake community language where people are told they matter but have no real room to build. The framework is meant to protect the seed of the world, not trap creators inside a tiny box.

If the universe grows beyond me, that means it worked.

## One-Line Founder Mission Statement

**I am building The World Beneath as a video game first, then opening it into a living IP where creators and fans can gather, create, sell, play, and grow the world together through story.**

## Short Pitch

**The World Beneath is a video game first, but the game is only the seed.**

It is a living IP built around the hidden version of our own world, where magic, aliens, secret orders, monsters, fairy tales, old conspiracies, ancient wars, and impossible forces are all real.

The first game establishes the world. The book and audiobook expand it. The Creator Kit, living canon archive, and AI coordinator give creators a way to build inside it without breaking it.

This is a new model for a video game IP: one founder plants the seed, then the community helps the world grow.

## The World Beneath Framework

The World Beneath is the world under the world.

It is the modern real world, but the hidden layer is true.

- Magic is real.
- Aliens are real.
- Secret organizations are real.
- Fairy tales are not fairy tales. They are buried history.
- Intent has power.
- Filth can manifest.
- Old orders, hidden wars, monsters, rituals, and impossible events exist beneath everyday life.

The setting is broad on purpose. A creator can tell a modern story, a period-piece story, a World War I trench warfare story, a horror comic, a mobile game, a visual novel, a lore book, a 3D print line, or something none of us have thought of yet.

The world is not one genre. It is a hidden reality that can hold many genres.

## Core Positioning

Use this sentence when explaining the whole project:

> **The World Beneath is a video game first, a living IP second, and a community engine at its core.**

Use this when explaining why it matters:

> **Community through story. A rising tide lifts all ships.**

## What Exists Before Kickstarter

The Kickstarter should not launch as an empty promise. It should launch with visible proof.

Before launch, the campaign should point to:

1. **The free Garden web app game** - released before the campaign as proof that the world is playable.
2. **The book** - proof that the world has story depth.
3. **The audiobook** - proof that the story can exist beyond the game.
4. **Trenchworks** - the first paid game/code reward and the strongest proof that the IP can hold different time periods.
5. **The canon framework** - enough rules to show that the world can scale.
6. **The first Creator Kit outline** - enough structure to show creators how they can build inside the IP.

## What Makes This Different

This is not Roblox, where creators build only inside a narrow platform.

This is not Minecraft, where community creativity mostly lives around the game.

This is closer to an open creative universe built from a video game seed.

Creators should be able to build whatever they can actually make: games, books, comics, visual novels, 3D prints, lore projects, mobile games, videos, and other formats. The living canon archive and AI coordinator keep everything from becoming chaos.

The goal is not just more content.

The goal is a real community where creators and fans can find each other again.
