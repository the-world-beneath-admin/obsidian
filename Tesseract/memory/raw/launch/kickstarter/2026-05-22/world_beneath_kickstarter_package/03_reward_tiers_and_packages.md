# Reward Tiers and Packages

## Reward Strategy

The campaign should not sell the free Garden game. Garden should be free before launch and used as proof that the world is real.

The paid reward path should begin with **Trenchworks**.

Use five clean core tiers, then add a few limited high-tier creator packages.

Kickstarter recommends keeping reward tiers simple, especially for a first campaign. So the main page should not feel like a giant menu.

## Core Reward Tiers

| Tier | Name | Reward |
|---:|---|---|

| Tier | Name | Reward |

## The $5,000 Lifetime Creator Pass

This is a good high-end tier, but it needs careful wording.

Use this wording:

> **The Lifetime Creator Pass gives you lifetime access to The World Beneath creator tools, Creator Kit updates, canon archive creator access, AI coordinator access within fair-use limits, and Founding Creator recognition, subject to the final Creator License and community rules.**

Do not say:

- lifetime ownership of the IP,
- guaranteed canon approval,
- unlimited AI usage forever,
- guaranteed profit,
- equity,
- revenue share,
- or permanent control over the world.

The pass should mean lifetime access to the creator system, not ownership of the company or guaranteed approval of any project.

## Steam / Trenchworks Delivery Wording

You said you do not know if Steam will be ready before the Kickstarter. That is fine if the reward is worded correctly.

Use this wording:

> **Trenchworks will be delivered as a digital game code. The intended platform is Steam if the Steam setup is ready in time. If Steam is not ready, backers will receive access through itch.io or direct digital delivery first, with Steam delivery pursued afterward where possible.**

This protects you from promising Steam keys before the Steam setup is complete.

Steam currently charges a $100 Direct fee per product, and first releases have timing/review steps. So do not make Steam the only promised delivery path unless the Steamworks setup is already complete.

## Add-Ons

Use add-ons sparingly.

Good add-ons:

- Extra digital book/audiobook copy.
- Extra Trenchworks code.
- Extra lore pack.
- Extra canon-fit report, limited quantity.
- Digital art/lore PDF bundle.

Avoid physical rewards unless you already have fulfillment solved.

Physical rewards can destroy a small campaign because shipping, taxes, packaging, delays, damage, and international delivery all become their own job.

## What Not to Offer

Do not offer:

- equity,
- financial returns,
- profit share,
- loans,
- unlimited commercial rights with no license terms,
- too many custom lore insertions,
- physical merch with uncertain costs,
- guaranteed canon status for backer ideas,
- or anything that forces you to personally serve hundreds of people one by one.

## Best Campaign Reward Stack

If the campaign page needs to be simple, show these as the main tiers:

1. $5 - Signal Backer
2. $25 - Story Backer
3. $35 - Trenchworks Backer
4. $60 - Founder Bundle
5. $100 - Creator Kit Early Access

Then show the $250, $500, $1,000, and $5,000 tiers as limited premium creator tiers.
