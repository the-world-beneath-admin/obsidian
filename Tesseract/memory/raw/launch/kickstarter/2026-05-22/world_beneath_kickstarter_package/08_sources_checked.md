# Sources Checked

These are the practical sources used to ground the Kickstarter and platform assumptions.

## Kickstarter Fees

Kickstarter states that if a project is successfully funded, it collects a 5% fee, and Stripe collects a payment processing fee of roughly 3-5%.

Source: https://help.kickstarter.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005028634-What-are-the-fees

## Kickstarter AI Disclosure

Kickstarter requires projects using AI tools to disclose what AI technology they plan to use, how AI-produced content will be incorporated, what parts are original work, and what elements use AI output. Projects developing AI tools/software must identify data sources and address consent/credit.

Source: https://help.kickstarter.com/hc/en-us/articles/16848396410267-Can-I-use-AI-generated-content-or-create-AI-technology-in-my-project

## Kickstarter Rewards

Kickstarter recommends limiting reward tiers, especially for a first Kickstarter project, and keeping rewards organized around schedule and budget.

Source: https://www.kickstarter.com/help/handbook/rewards

## Kickstarter Stretch Goals

Kickstarter says stretch goals are not part of the official platform and can add complexity, extra costs, and fulfillment risk.

Source: https://help.kickstarter.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005128274-What-are-stretch-goals

## Kickstarter Backers Are Not Investors

Kickstarter says project creators keep ownership of their work, and Kickstarter cannot be used to offer equity, financial returns, or loans.

Source: https://help.kickstarter.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005047953-What-do-backers-get-in-return

## Steam Direct

Steamworks says the Steam Direct fee is $100 per product/app, and the process includes paperwork, bank/tax information, review, and timing requirements for first releases.

Sources:

- https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/gettingstarted/appfee
- https://partner.steamgames.com/steamdirect
