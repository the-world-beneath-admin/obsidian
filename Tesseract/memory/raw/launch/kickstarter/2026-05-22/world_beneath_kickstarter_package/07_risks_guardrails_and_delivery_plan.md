# Risks, Guardrails, and Delivery Plan

## The Biggest Risk

The biggest risk is overpromising.

The idea is big. The campaign promise needs to be controlled.

Backers should feel the full vision, but the actual deliverables must be Phase One deliverables.

## Guardrail 1: Do Not Promise Unlimited IP Freedom

Do not say:

> Anyone can do anything with the IP forever.

Say:

> Creators will be able to build under The World Beneath through a clear creator license, canon guidelines, approved assets, and review rules. The goal is real creative freedom inside a protected framework.

You need an attorney to draft the final creator license.

## Guardrail 2: Do Not Offer Equity Or Financial Returns

Kickstarter should not be framed as an investment.

Backers can receive rewards, access, credits, digital content, creator tools, and experiences.

They should not be offered ownership, equity, profit share, financial returns, or loans.

## Guardrail 3: Be Clear About AI

The project should disclose AI use clearly.

Suggested campaign language:

> **This project uses AI as a creative-assistance, planning, coding-assistance, and canon-coordination tool. The AI coordinator is designed to help creators compare project ideas against The World Beneath canon archive and receive suggestions. It does not replace creator ownership, human review, or final canon approval.**

Also disclose:

- what AI tools you expect to use,
- what parts of the project use AI output,
- what parts are original founder-created work,
- what data the AI coordinator references,
- how creator submissions are handled,
- and how consent/credit will work for creator-submitted material.

## Guardrail 4: Keep Physical Rewards Out At First

Physical rewards are dangerous for a one-person company.

Avoid:

- shirts,
- posters,
- boxed editions,
- minis,
- 3D printed shipments,
- international packages,
- signed physical books,
- complicated merch.

Use digital rewards first.

Physical products can come later through a store or partner after the IP/community is working.

## Guardrail 5: Steam Fallback

Do not promise Steam-only delivery unless Steam setup is complete.

Use this structure:

- intended platform: Steam,
- fallback platform: itch.io/direct digital download,
- Steam pursued after setup/review where possible.

This lets you still offer Trenchworks codes without being trapped by platform timing.

## Guardrail 6: Stretch Goal Control

World Key minigames are good stretch goals only if they stay small.

Each World Key should be:

- web-based,
- limited in scope,
- one clear mechanic,
- one canon theme,
- one small loop,
- no huge multiplayer promises,
- no giant 3D system,
- no endless progression system.

## Suggested Delivery Timeline

### Before Kickstarter Launch

- Release Garden web app for free.
- Prepare campaign page and video.
- Prepare playable/media proof for Trenchworks.
- Build basic Creator Kit outline.
- Build canon archive structure.
- Define AI coordinator prototype behavior.
- Draft creator license outline with attorney or legal professional.
- Set up Discord/community hub.

### Month 1 After Funding

- Funds arrive.
- Finalize budget.
- Hire only critical contractors.
- Begin legal/license work.
- Publish backer roadmap.

### Months 2-3

- Trenchworks production push.
- Creator Kit v1 draft.
- Canon archive v1 internal build.
- AI coordinator prototype planning/build.
- Community onboarding.

### Months 4-6

- Trenchworks backer access target window, if ready.
- Creator Kit v1 release to creator tiers.
- Canon archive preview.
- First creator feedback cycle.

### Months 7-9

- AI coordinator prototype release to creator tiers.
- Canon-fit workshop fulfillment.
- First World Key stretch goal work if unlocked.

### Months 10-12

- Stretch goal delivery window begins.
- Creator showcase planning.
- Book/audiobook stretch delivery if unlocked and scoped.
- Public report on Phase One and next steps.

## Risks and Challenges Copy For Kickstarter

Use something like this:

> **The biggest challenge is scope. The long-term vision for The World Beneath is large, but this Kickstarter is specifically for Phase One. I am keeping the base deliverables focused: Trenchworks, the Creator Kit, the living canon archive, the AI coordinator prototype, and the community launch system. Stretch goals will be limited to small digital World Key minigames, books, archive expansions, and creator-tool upgrades so the project does not collapse under its own ambition.**

> **I am also treating Steam delivery carefully. Trenchworks is intended for digital code delivery, with Steam as the preferred path if ready. If Steam setup is not ready, I will use itch.io or direct digital delivery as the fallback.**

> **Because this project uses AI tools, I will disclose how AI is used, what remains original founder-created work, and how the AI coordinator references canon material. The AI coordinator is a creator-assistance tool, not a replacement for human creativity or final canon review.**

## Final Rule

The dream can be huge.

The Kickstarter promise must be shippable.
