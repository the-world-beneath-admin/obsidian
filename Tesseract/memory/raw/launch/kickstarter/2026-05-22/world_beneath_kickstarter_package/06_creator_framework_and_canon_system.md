# Creator Framework and Canon System

## The Core Problem

AI is going to make it easier for people to create games, books, comics, art, lore, music, models, and worlds.

But creation alone is not enough.

Creators still need outreach. They need a place where people actually see their work, talk about it, buy it, and care about it.

Fans have the opposite problem. They have endless content, but fewer real communities where they can belong and help something grow.

The World Beneath should bring creators and fans back together.

## The Double-Sided Loop

The loop is simple:

1. The game brings fans into the world.
2. The community gives fans a place to gather.
3. The Creator Kit lets creators build inside the world.
4. Creators make more things for fans to consume.
5. More content gives fans more reason to stay.
6. More fans give creators more reason to build.
7. The living canon archive and AI coordinator keep it all connected.

This is the engine.

## The Creator Kit

The Creator Kit should give people a practical way to create inside The World Beneath.

Creator Kit v1 should include:

- world premise,
- tone guide,
- basic canon rules,
- timeline rules,
- factions overview,
- creature rules,
- intent/magic rules,
- filth manifestation rules,
- secret organization guidelines,
- period-piece guidelines,
- modern-setting guidelines,
- asset access rules,
- what creators can make,
- what requires review,
- what is off-limits,
- how to submit projects,
- how projects are listed or recognized,
- commercial creator license overview.

## The Living Canon Archive

The living canon archive is the memory of the world.

It should track:

- canon rules,
- factions,
- eras,
- major events,
- locations,
- creatures,
- artifacts,
- magic/intent systems,
- filth phenomena,
- secret organizations,
- creator project status,
- approved references,
- contradictions and corrections,
- open story hooks.

The archive lets the world grow without falling apart.

Without the archive, every new creator adds confusion.

With the archive, every new creator can add value.

## The AI Canon Coordinator

The AI coordinator should be framed as a lore assistant, not as a boss.

Its job is to help creators answer questions like:

- Does my idea fit The World Beneath?
- What existing canon does it connect to?
- What does it accidentally break?
- What changes would make it fit better?
- Is this project a side story, canon candidate, or independent creator project?
- What time period would work best?
- What factions could be involved?
- What risks should I avoid?

## AI Coordinator Output Format

When a creator submits an idea, the AI coordinator should return something like this:

1. **Canon Fit Score** - low, medium, high.
2. **What Works** - what already fits the world.
3. **Canon Conflicts** - what breaks or strains the world.
4. **Suggested Fixes** - how to adjust the idea.
5. **Relevant Canon** - factions, eras, rules, creatures, locations.
6. **Project Category** - fan project, creator project, canon candidate, or needs review.
7. **Next Steps** - what the creator should do next.

## Creator Project Categories

Use simple categories:

### Fan Project

Non-commercial or casual community project.

### Creator Project

Commercial or serious project made under the Creator License.

### Canon Candidate

A creator project that might become recognized in the official archive after review.

### Official Canon

Founder-approved or company-approved material that becomes part of the core archive.

### Non-Canon But Allowed

A project that can exist as an alternate story, parody, experiment, or creator-side project, but does not define the world.

## Creator Freedom With Guardrails

The right message is:

> **The framework protects the world without suffocating the creator.**

Creators should have real room to build.

But the IP still needs rules:

- no guaranteed canon approval,
- no uncontrolled use of core trademarks without license terms,
- no hate content,
- no scams,
- no projects that break the world beyond repair,
- no pretending to be official without approval,
- no selling unapproved assets as if they are official company products,
- no creator ownership claim over the entire IP.

## Founder Role

The founder role should be stated clearly:

- You are the seed founder.
- You own and operate the starting company/IP framework.
- You create the first game, book, audiobook, and rules.
- You hire contractors as needed.
- You open the door to creators through a license.
- You do not pretend the company is a committee.
- You do not fake community control.
- You build a system where community creation can become real.

## The Center Of The Project

The center is not technology.

The center is not even the game.

The center is:

# Community through story.

The game brings people in.

The world gives them a shared language.

The Creator Kit gives them a way to build.

The canon archive keeps the world coherent.

The AI coordinator makes the system usable.

The community makes it matter.
