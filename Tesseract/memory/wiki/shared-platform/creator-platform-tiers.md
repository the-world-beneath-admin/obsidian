# Creator Platform Tiers

## Status

User-defined business direction recorded on 2026-05-22. This is not final public legal/policy copy yet.

## Scope

This page covers The World Beneath creator platform tier structure for website/shared-platform planning.

## Current Tier Direction

Tier 1:

- `$20/month` subscription.
- Intended as the lower creator-platform access tier.

Tier 2:

- For anyone who creates a company with gross earnings over `$100,000` per year.
- Intended as the higher creator/company tier.
- Business intent: if someone materially profits from TWB-derived creator-platform use, Bob wants more than the flat `$20/month` subscription. The preferred structure should capture a small percentage from high-earning commercial use so that very large successes return value to the project owner.

## Preferred Model Direction

Use revenue-share / commercial license language rather than informal "profit share" language.

Reason:

- Profit can be obscured by expenses, salaries, accounting choices, and company structure.
- Gross revenue attributable to TWB-derived commercial products is easier to define and audit.
- A threshold plus small percentage is cleaner than charging every hobby creator.

Possible structure to explore:

- Tier 1: `$20/month` for hobbyists, early creators, and small commercial experiments below the commercial threshold.
- Tier 2: creator/company commercial license required after crossing a defined revenue threshold.
- Tier 2 payment: small percentage of TWB-attributable gross revenue above the threshold, or a minimum subscription plus percentage, pending legal/accounting review.
- Keep the threshold focused on revenue connected to TWB-derived work, unless Bob intentionally wants total company revenue to trigger the tier.

## Boundary

Do not publish this directly as legal, billing, or creator-policy copy until the public wording is reviewed.

Do not implement Stripe/product enforcement from this note alone. It is a durable direction, not a complete billing contract.

## Open Questions

- Does Tier 2 trigger on trailing 12-month gross revenue, calendar-year gross revenue, tax-year gross revenue, or self-reported current annualized revenue?
- Is the `$100,000/year` threshold for total company gross earnings, TWB-related gross earnings, or creator-business gross earnings?
- What proof, if any, is required for Tier 2?
- Is Tier 2 a separate subscription, a revenue-share/commercial license tier, a manual agreement, or an account flag?
- What percentage should apply, and should it apply only to revenue above the threshold or all covered revenue once the threshold is crossed?
- What happens when a creator crosses the threshold mid-year?
- What happens if a creator drops below the threshold later?
- What benefits, limits, marketplace rights, support level, or licensing rights distinguish Tier 1 from Tier 2?
- What public language should be used for compliance, taxes, refunds, cancellation, and creator obligations?
