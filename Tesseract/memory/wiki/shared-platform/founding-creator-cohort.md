# Founding Creator Cohort

## Status

Draft offer and selection structure created on 2026-05-22. Not final legal/policy copy.

## Purpose

Recruit the first `10` independent creators who want to build around The World Beneath IP and can show evidence that they can actually produce.

## Offer

The first `10` selected independent creators receive lifetime access to the planned `$20/month` Creator Access tier for free.

This means:

- no monthly Tier 1 Creator Access fee
- early creator-platform updates and working notes
- a direct lane to discuss what they want to build
- a chance to help shape creator-platform rules before they harden

This does not mean:

- employment
- investment
- canon approval
- official partnership
- endorsement
- compensation
- exclusive rights
- unlimited commercial use
- a waiver of Tier 2 commercial licensing if they later earn serious revenue from TWB-derived commercial work

## Who Fits

Open to independent creators such as:

- streamers
- bloggers
- writers
- video creators
- 3D print labs
- miniature painters
- prop makers
- artists
- modders
- map makers
- tool builders
- community organizers

## Selection Standard

Applicants need proof, not just an idea.

Acceptable proof:

- completed video, article, story, model, print, campaign, mod, tool, shop listing, portfolio, or other finished work
- channel, blog, store, or archive that shows consistent output
- photos or evidence of relevant equipment when the idea depends on equipment
- a production pipeline or work samples that make the proposed TWB project believable

Good applications answer:

- Who are you?
- What kind of creator are you?
- What have you already completed?
- What equipment, skills, or production process do you have?
- What do you want to build with The World Beneath?
- What is the smallest first version you could produce?
- Why does it fit TWB?

## Review Rubric

Score each applicant from `0` to `3`:

- Production proof: has finished work or real evidence of capability
- Fit with TWB: understands the tone, world, and audience
- Specificity: has a focused first project, not vague enthusiasm
- Reliability: shows signs they can complete work without constant rescue
- Risk: low legal/reputation/IP confusion risk

Recommended acceptance:

- strong accept: `12+` total and no major risk flag
- maybe: `9-11` total or one fixable concern
- decline: under `9`, no proof of production, or major risk flag
