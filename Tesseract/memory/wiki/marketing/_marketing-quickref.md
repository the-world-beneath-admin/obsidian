# Marketing Quickref

## Current Positioning

- Fact - The World Beneath is the parent/main game.
- Signal - Public main-game copy frames it as a shared-world RPG centered on dungeon runs, creature growth, crafting, inventory, societies, trade, territory pressure, and community/account identity.
- Decision - Main-game positioning should lead with the concrete RPG loop first, then frame it as a shared-world RPG.
- Decision - Landing-page strategy should lead visitors toward playing the first free World Keys while they wait for the full game.
- Decision - First public free World Keys are The Garden and The Alchemy Lab.
- Approved Working Pitch - The World Beneath is a shared-world RPG where dungeon runs feed creature growth, crafting, inventory progression, and a larger hidden world shaped by player societies.
- Approved Copy Foundation - "Run dungeons. Grow creatures. Shape the world beneath."
- Current Landing Direction - "Play the World Keys now while you wait for the full game."
- Backup Lane - Use "creature-growth dungeon RPG" as a store/page/campaign test lane, not as approved primary positioning yet.

## Target Audience

- Hypothesis - Players who like dungeon RPGs, creature collectors, crafting/progression RPGs, monster/team building, and long-run account progression.
- Hypothesis - Players who want a world/community to join before the full game arrives.
- Hypothesis - Existing lore readers/listeners who want the game expression of the setting.
- Hypothesis - Idle/incremental RPG players may be adjacent if the main game foregrounds offline or long-run progression, but this needs validation.

## SEO Direction

- Main-game keyword clusters: shared-world RPG, creature collector RPG, dungeon crawler RPG, crafting RPG, monster collection game, party-based RPG, progression RPG, fantasy RPG, community RPG, creature growth game.
- Candidate tag hypotheses: RPG, Dungeon Crawler, Creature Collector, Party-Based RPG, Crafting, Loot, Inventory Management, Strategy RPG, Progression, Fantasy, Community, Shared World.
- Adjacent but unapproved: Idler, Automation, Incremental, MMO.

## Recent Decisions

- Decision - Main-game SEO and Glassroot SEO must remain distinct.
- Decision - Marketing should anchor main-game claims to current public copy and main-game authority docs.
- Decision - Main-game positioning uses concrete loop first, shared-world frame second.
- Decision - The website front door should make playable World Keys the primary CTA once the first public keys are ready; book and audiobook are deeper story/lore paths.

## Warnings

- Warning - Do not treat Glassroot SEO as main-game SEO.
- Warning - Do not use Glassroot Garden as the public World Key name; use The Garden unless the user changes the naming decision.
- Warning - Do not claim full gameplay systems are live when public state is still community/account/lore plus developing game systems.
- Warning - Do not over-position as MMO, idle, or creature collector unless the chosen build and store strategy supports that promise.
- Warning - Do not bury the actual playable center under lore-only language.
- Warning - Do not make the homepage imply the full main game is playable if the live product is a World Key.
