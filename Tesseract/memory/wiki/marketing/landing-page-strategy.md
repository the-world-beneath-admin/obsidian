# Landing Page Strategy

## Summary

The public landing page should move visitors toward playing the first free World Keys while they wait for the full game.

The book and audiobook should remain available as deeper story/lore paths, but they should not be the primary landing-page conversion unless a campaign specifically targets readers.

## Current Decision

- Decision - 2026-05-11 - Make "play the World Keys now while you wait for the main game" the primary landing-page direction.
  - Reason: The first two World Keys are expected to be the first player-facing ready products in the game world. They give visitors something playable and concrete before the full game is ready.
  - Source: User direction - 2026-05-11.
  - Source: [[wiki/launch/world-keys-kickstarter-itch-readiness-plan]]
- Decision - 2026-05-11 - Name the first two public free World Keys **The Garden** and **The Alchemy Lab**.
  - Reason: The public names should be simple and personal, while players name their own garden/lab inside the experience.
  - Source: User clarification - 2026-05-11.
  - Source: [[wiki/launch/first-two-world-keys-release-briefs]]

## Page Hierarchy

1. Primary conversion: play the available World Keys.
2. Secondary conversion: follow or register for The World Beneath.
3. Tertiary conversion: read or listen deeper into the story through the book and audiobook.
4. Supporting conversion: follow Kickstarter or Steam when those pages are ready.

## Draft Landing Promise

Candidate direction:

> Play The Garden and The Alchemy Lab while The World Beneath grows.

Supporting copy:

> The World Beneath is a shared-world RPG in development. While the full game takes shape, free World Keys let you step into smaller playable corners of the world, name your own spaces, and follow the path toward the deeper game.

CTA direction:

> Play the World Keys

Secondary CTA direction:

> Follow The World Beneath

Lore CTA direction:

> Read deeper into the story

## Website Implications

- Homepage hero should foreground the playable World Keys once at least one public key is ready.
- The current approved main-game pitch still matters, but it should support the play-now CTA rather than compete with it.
- The World Keys page should become a stronger release hub with individual key cards, status, platform links, screenshots, and "play free" CTAs.
- The first public World Key cards should be The Garden and The Alchemy Lab.
- Book and audiobook links should move into a lore/story section, a lower homepage band, or a dedicated story page.
- Kickstarter copy should point at playable World Keys as proof, not treat them as locked rewards.
- Steam copy should continue to position the main game and collect wishlist intent once the Steam page exists.

## Warnings

- Warning - Do not make the homepage sound like the full main game is already playable if only the World Keys are playable.
- Warning - Do not bury the playable World Keys below lore, book, audiobook, or general project explanation once they are public.
- Warning - Do not call the World Keys a demo unless that is the intended positioning.
- Warning - Do not promise that World Key progress carries into the main game unless the system exists.

## Open Questions

- Answered - First public World Key: The Garden.
- Answered - Second public World Key: The Alchemy Lab.
- Open Question - Should the homepage say "World Keys" immediately, or use a clearer public phrase such as "free playable chapters" until visitors understand the term?
- Open Question - Should the first World Key CTA point to itch.io directly, or to a website hub that then links to itch.io?
