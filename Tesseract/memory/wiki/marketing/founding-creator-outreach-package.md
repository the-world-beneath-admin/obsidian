# Founding Creator Outreach Package

## Status

Draft outreach copy created on 2026-05-22.

## Core Offer

The World Beneath is looking for the first `10` independent creators who can show proof they can produce and want to build around the IP.

Selected creators receive lifetime access to the planned `$20/month` Creator Access tier for free.

Important boundary:

- This is creator-platform access, not employment, investment, canon approval, official partnership, endorsement, or unlimited commercial licensing.
- Commercial Tier 2 rules still apply if the creator later earns serious TWB-derived commercial revenue.

## One-Line Pitch

The World Beneath is looking for 10 independent creators with proof they can produce: writers, streamers, bloggers, 3D print labs, artists, modders, and builders who want early lifetime Creator Access.

## Application Checklist

Ask each applicant for:

- name / handle
- email
- creator type
- completed work links
- equipment or production-process proof
- proposed TWB project
- smallest first version
- why it fits The World Beneath
- any commercial intent

## Screening Reply - Needs More Proof

```text
Thanks for sending this in.

The idea may fit, but I need more proof that you can produce it before I can consider you for one of the first 10 creator slots.

Useful proof would be completed work, portfolio links, a channel or shop, photos of relevant equipment, or a clear example of your production process.
```

## Screening Reply - Not A Fit

```text
Thanks for applying.

I am keeping the first creator cohort very small and focused on people who can show production proof and a tight fit with The World Beneath. I am not moving this application forward right now, but I appreciate you taking the time to send it.
```

## Acceptance Reply

```text
Thanks for applying.

I would like to include you in the first founding creator cohort for The World Beneath.

That means lifetime access to the planned $20/month Creator Access tier once the creator platform is active. This does not create employment, partnership, canon approval, endorsement, or a waiver of future commercial licensing rules, but it does give you an early creator lane and a direct place to shape what you want to build.

Next step: send me the smallest first version of what you want to make and what you need from the TWB side to begin.
```

## Best Places To Use

- Kickstarter updates
- Discord announcement
- creator-policy page link
- contact page link

- TWB-Marketing Daily Queue as a manual campaign item

## Do Not Say

- "Partner"
- "Official canon creator"
- "Guaranteed income"
- "Investment opportunity"
- "Revenue share for backers"
- "Unlimited commercial license"
- "You can make anything with the IP forever"
