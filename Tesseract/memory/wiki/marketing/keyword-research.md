# Keyword Research

## Summary

Initial main-game keyword clusters are hypotheses based on public TWB copy, local authority docs, and a light comparator scan. They need validation with search volume, Steam/store search behavior, page tests, and campaign data.

## Memory Items

- Hypothesis - Core cluster: shared-world RPG, dungeon RPG, creature RPG, crafting RPG, progression RPG.
- Hypothesis - Creature cluster: creature collector RPG, monster collector game, creature growth game, pet RPG, party-based RPG.
- Hypothesis - Dungeon/progression cluster: dungeon crawler RPG, dungeon runs, loot RPG, inventory RPG, crafting and dungeon game.
- Hypothesis - Community/world cluster: community RPG, shared world game, player societies game, territory control RPG, world map RPG.
- Hypothesis - Lore/brand cluster: The World Beneath, World Beneath game, hidden world RPG, lore-rich RPG.
- Hypothesis - Adjacent cluster: idle RPG, incremental RPG, automation RPG, MMO-lite RPG. Use cautiously.
- Warning - "Creature collector" may overpromise if collecting is not the first public center.
- Warning - "MMO" may attract the wrong expectation if societies/shared-world systems are not yet live at MMO scale.
- Warning - "Idle" should remain adjacent unless the product explicitly markets idle/offline progression.
