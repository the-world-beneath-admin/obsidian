# Trailer Hooks

## Summary

Main-game trailer hooks should show the core RPG loop quickly: dungeon pressure, creature identity, rewards, crafting/progression, and the promise of a larger shared world.

## Memory Items

- Hypothesis - Best cold-open visual: ordinary world surface gives way to the hidden World Beneath, then cuts quickly to dungeon danger.
- Hypothesis - Best mechanic hook: dungeon run reward becomes creature growth, crafting input, or inventory progression.
- Hypothesis - Best world hook: player societies and territory pressure imply a world bigger than a private grind.
- Warning - Do not lead with lore before the viewer understands what they do.
- Warning - Do not show World Key subgame footage as if it is the main-game loop.
