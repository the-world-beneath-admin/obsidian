# Wishlist Strategy

## Summary

Main-game wishlist/store strategy has not been validated yet. The first useful store-facing goal is to identify which hook earns attention: dungeon runs, creature growth, crafting/progression, shared world, or community-first development.

## Memory Items

- Decision - Wishlist/store copy should lead with the concrete loop before dense lore.
- Decision - Approved primary lane is concrete loop first, shared-world RPG frame second.
- Decision - Before Steam is live, the owned website should point players toward free World Keys as the play-now conversion.
- Decision - The first play-now CTAs can name The Garden and The Alchemy Lab directly.
- Hypothesis - The strongest early capsule/copy test may be the approved working pitch versus "creature-growth dungeon RPG".
- Open Question - Which hook produces better wishlist/follow/account intent from cold players?
- Open Question - Does a World Keys-first homepage create stronger follow/register intent than a general main-game development page?
- Open Question - Should the homepage have one "Play the World Keys" CTA or separate CTAs for The Garden and The Alchemy Lab?
- Warning - Do not optimize for wishlists before the store or game page can honestly show the supported loop.
- Warning - Do not let Steam/Kickstarter CTAs replace the play-now World Keys CTA before those platform pages are ready.
