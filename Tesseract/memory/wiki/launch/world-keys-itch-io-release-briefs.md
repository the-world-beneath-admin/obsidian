## Shared Release Position

- Decision - The first two World Keys should be free to play.
- Decision - The website remains the owned hub: https://the-world-beneath.com/world-keys/
- Decision - Use separate itch.io pages for The Garden and The Alchemy Lab unless a later publishing decision bundles them.
- Warning - Do not call either release the full game.
- Warning - Do not promise account sync, unlocks, save carryover, or main-game import unless those systems are working.
- Warning - Do not rely on empty placeholder pages for discovery. itch.io indexing expects a public page with cover art and playable, downloadable, or purchasable content.
- Warning - The public title "The Garden" is player-friendly but generic for search. Keep the title, but use "World Key" in the page subtitle, short description, devlog, and URL slug.

### Long Description Draft

The Garden is a free World Key for The World Beneath.

Open the key, name your own garden, and begin tending a small living space connected to the larger world we are building. This is not the full shared-world RPG. It is an early player-facing piece of that world: a focused browser experience built around care, growth, discovery, and the feeling of owning a strange little place beneath the surface.

The full game is still in development. The Garden is here so players can start interacting with the world now while they wait.

### Player Fantasy

The player opens a private garden through a World Key, names it, tends growth, and starts forming a personal attachment to a place inside The World Beneath.

### Working Core Loop Target

1. Name the garden.
2. Tend or inspect the current growing space.
3. Make a small choice about growth, care, or use of resources.
4. See a readable result.
5. Return with a reason to keep shaping the garden.

This is a target loop, not a confirmed final feature list.

### What To Say Publicly

- Free World Key.

- First playable garden-facing entry into The World Beneath.
- Players name their own garden.
- The full game is still in development.

## Recommended Release Order

Release The Garden first, then The Alchemy Lab.

Reason:

- The Garden has the clearest current public hook.
- The Garden has existing prototype/source lineage, even though the public name changed.
- A staggered launch gives each World Key room for a devlog, social push, website update, and learning pass.

Do not launch both on the same day unless both are stable, both have finished assets, and the team can support both pages at once.

## Ready To Publish When

- The uploaded ZIP runs on itch.io from `index.html`.
- All asset paths are relative and case-correct.
- The page is public or ready for public switch.
- Cover image is uploaded.
- Screenshots or GIFs show the actual playable experience.
- Controls are listed.
- Known issues are listed honestly.
- Accessibility notes are present.
- Free pricing choice is set intentionally.
- The page links back to https://the-world-beneath.com/world-keys/
- No unsupported account sync, unlock, carryover, or full-game promises remain.
