## Core Recommendation

Public Kickstarter base goal:

```text
$150,000
```

Strategic framing:

> Help launch Phase One of The World Beneath: a free proof-of-world web game, Trenchworks as the first paid game reward, the Creator Kit, the living canon archive, the AI coordinator, and the first real community system for a creator-grown IP.

## Mission Positioning

Core sentence:

> The World Beneath is a video game first, a living IP second, and a community engine at its core.

Founder mission:

> I am building The World Beneath as a video game first, then opening it into a living IP where creators and fans can gather, create, sell, play, and grow the world together through story.

Campaign hook:

> This is a video game first. But the game is only the seed.

## Phase One Promise

The Kickstarter promise must stay shippable. Phase One funds:

- one year of founder runway
- Trenchworks delivery
- Creator Kit v1
- living canon archive v1
- AI canon coordinator prototype
- community hub / creator-fan loop
- legal/IP setup for creator participation
- critical tools, cloud, AI, and operating reserves

Do not pitch the campaign as an unlimited dream. Pitch the larger dream, but promise Phase One.

## Proof Before Launch

Kickstarter should not launch as an empty promise. Before launch, the public funnel should point to:

- The Garden as the free playable proof-of-world web app
- book proof / story foundation
- audiobook proof / expanded medium
- Trenchworks visible enough to serve as first paid game/code reward
- Creator Kit outline
- canon framework outline
- AI coordinator prototype behavior outline
- community hub path

## Mandatory Guardrails

- Do not offer equity, financial returns, revenue share to backers, or loans.
- Do not promise unlimited IP freedom.
- Do not imply backers own part of TWB.
- Do not make Steam the only promised Trenchworks delivery path until Steam setup is complete.
- Do not add physical rewards unless fulfillment is solved.
- Disclose AI use clearly.
- Keep stretch goals scoped to small digital World Keys, books/novellas, archive expansion, and creator tools.
- Creator rewards are access/review/community rewards, not canon approval or commercial free-for-all.

## Current External Checks

Checked live official sources on 2026-05-22:

- Kickstarter fees: 5% platform fee if funded; Stripe processing roughly 3-5%.
- Kickstarter AI projects/content must disclose tools, incorporation, originality, and data/consent details when building AI tools.
- Kickstarter backers cannot be offered equity, financial returns, or loans.
- Kickstarter stretch goals are unofficial platform constructs and add complexity/cost risk.
- Steam Direct fee is `$100` per app; Steam setup includes paperwork, tax/bank info, review/timing, and fee recoupment only after sufficient adjusted gross revenue.
