# TWB-Marketing Safety And Platform Rules

## Status

Active safety boundary - created 2026-05-12.

## Core Rule

TWB-Marketing may assist with drafting, scheduling plans, tracking, review, and preparation. It must not perform risky or deceptive platform behavior.

## Allowed For Now

- Draft posts for user review.
- Track campaign ideas, draft status, platform readiness, and approval state.
- Store copy, assets, notes, links, and manual posting checklists.
- Prepare forum/social outreach plans with clear source notes and human review.
- Record analytics or performance notes manually or from approved exports later.
- Use local draft assistance, including local Gemma drafting, when outputs remain review-only.
- Store personal local credentials on the user's PC only when handled by the local encrypted password vault.

## Not Allowed Yet

- Auto-posting to social, forum, store, or community platforms.
- Fixed-interval posting or engagement such as posting every 10 minutes.
- Connecting social or forum accounts.
- Automating social or forum account login.
- Using platform APIs before platform rules, account permissions, and approval flow are reviewed.
- Spam campaigns, bulk unsolicited posting, engagement farming, fake replies, fake votes, fake reviews, or synthetic community activity.
- Disguising advertisements as neutral help or using hashtags/wording tricks to evade no-advertising rules.
- Scraping private, gated, logged-in, or non-public data.
- Evading platform limits, moderation systems, rate limits, or community rules.
- Treating the password vault as a shared/team credential system without a proper security plan.

## Future Integration Gate

Before any integration or scheduled posting feature is considered, the orchestrator must require:

1. Named platform and target workflow.
2. Official platform rules or API terms reviewed.
3. Account permission model documented.
4. Explicit user approval for the integration plan.
5. Manual review and final approval step preserved before posting.
6. Safe failure behavior documented.

## Memory Items

- Decision - Manual approval is a required design principle for any posting workflow.
- Decision - Platform integrations are blocked until reviewed against official rules and account permissions.
- Decision - The safe agentic path is opportunity discovery, rule-risk review, draft assistance, disclosure support, and approval queueing.
- Decision - Local Gemma drafting is acceptable only as review-only local assistance.
- Decision - The password vault is personal/local-only unless a future security review approves a broader model.
- Warning - Do not let convenience features blur into spam, impersonation, or fake engagement.
- Warning - Do not build or hand off an agent whose purpose is fixed-interval posting, disguised advertising, or platform-rule evasion.
- Warning - Do not let "daily queue" become pressure for daily posting when a lane is held, blocked, or missing assets.
