# World Key Pets

## Summary

World Keys can share umbrella pet concepts, but individual games use pets differently.

## Current Facts

- In The Garden, pets/Companions automate farming work.
- In The Alchemy Lab, pets go into the cave and retrieve cave materials.
- Pets should be shared account companions across World Keys and the main game.
- Current Alchemy pets release from the shed one at a time with a 30-second interval.
- In The Alchemy Lab, sleep cooldown begins after the pet returns to the shed and unloads, not when energy reaches zero.

## Rules

- Do not assume one World Key pet behavior applies globally.
- Pet identities are account-level, while levels, cooldowns, and per-game behavior may be global or per-mini-game; this remains unresolved.
- Do not connect pet state to a production account model until the shared service contract is designed.

## Open Questions

- Should pet identities, levels, and cooldowns be shared globally across World Keys or per mini-game?
- Should pet automation language remain "Companion" in all World Keys, or vary by game surface?
- How should companion locks prevent the same pet from doing incompatible activities across World Keys?
