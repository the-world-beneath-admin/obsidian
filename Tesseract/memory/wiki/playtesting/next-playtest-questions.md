# Next Playtest Questions

## Purpose

Scope: World Key: Glassroot Garden playtesting.

Use the first Glassroot Garden playtests to validate whether players understand the core loop, enjoy Companion automation, and can move through the farm-to-processing flow without a guided explanation.

## Primary Questions

- Open Question - Can a new player explain what the game is after 3 minutes?
- Open Question - Can a new player plant a crop without being told the exact controls?
- Open Question - Does the player understand that Companions till, fetch, plant, and harvest?
- Open Question - Does the player notice optional work windows?
- Open Question - Does the player understand how optional work changes yield or quality?
- Open Question - Can the player move harvested raw herbs to drying?
- Open Question - Can the player move dried herbs into the Bundler in recipe order?
- Open Question - Does the player understand the difference between Notice Board contracts and World Key shared storage?
- Open Question - Which moment feels most satisfying?
- Open Question - Which moment creates the first confusion or hesitation?

## Watch Closely

- Seed Bag opening and crop selection.
- Seed-to-plot input.
- Companion work-stage feedback.
- Optional work-window visibility.
- Raw bin and dried bin labels.
- Drying rack interaction.
- Bundler recipe order.
- Finished bundle timer and resolution.
- Contract reward versus transfer bundle destination.

## Do Not Conclude Yet

- Do not claim a mechanic is loved from one polite comment.
- Do not claim a mechanic is confusing from one mistimed click.
- Do not promote internal browser verification as player feedback.
