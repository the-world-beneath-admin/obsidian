# Confusion Points

## Summary

Scope: World Key: Glassroot Garden playtesting.

No confirmed player confusion points have been captured yet.

The items below are predicted risks from the current design and prototype flow. They must be validated through playtesting before becoming signals.

## Memory Items

- Hypothesis - Players may not understand why dropping a seed queues Companion work instead of planting instantly.
- Hypothesis - Players may miss optional work windows if the visual state is too subtle.
- Hypothesis - Players may not understand the difference between raw herbs, dried herbs, finished bundles, contracts, and World Key shared storage.
- Hypothesis - Players may expect drag/drop to be mandatory and miss click fallbacks, or vice versa.
- Hypothesis - Players may read Companion failures as punishment unless the result and tone are clearly harmless.
- Hypothesis - Players may confuse Notice Board contract rewards with account-level World Key storage transfers.

## Evidence Needed

- Where the player hesitates.
- What the player clicks incorrectly.
- What the player asks aloud.
- Which UI labels they misread.
- Whether confusion resolves naturally or requires explanation.
