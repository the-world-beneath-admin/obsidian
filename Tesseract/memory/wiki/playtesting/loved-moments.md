# Loved Moments

## Summary

Scope: World Key: Glassroot Garden playtesting.

No confirmed player-loved moments have been captured yet.

The items below are candidate moments to watch during the first playtests, not evidence.

## Memory Items

- Hypothesis - Players may enjoy seeing Companions visibly till, fetch seeds, plant crops, and harvest without direct micromanagement.
- Hypothesis - Companion success/failure animations may become a memorable personality hook if failures are funny rather than punishing.
- Hypothesis - The first completed crop-to-contract loop may be satisfying because it turns a visible farm action into a clear reward.
- Hypothesis - The magitech Bundler may become a strong tactile moment if recipe loading, machine activation, and finished bundle timing are clear.
- Hypothesis - The black-glass / alchemical greenhouse fantasy may be a differentiator if the game avoids looking like a generic cozy farm.

## Evidence Needed

- Exact player quotes.
- Observed moments where players smile, laugh, pause to inspect, or voluntarily repeat an action.
- Moments players describe later without prompting.
