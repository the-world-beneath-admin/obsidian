## Proposed Direction

- Tier 0: community/noncommercial participation.
- Tier 1: `$20/month` Creator Access for small creators and early experiments.
- Tier 2: Commercial Creator License after crossing `$100,000` in TWB-attributable covered gross revenue over a trailing 12-month period.
- Initial royalty candidate: `2%` of covered gross revenue above `$100,000`, paid quarterly.
- Custom/enterprise review once covered gross revenue exceeds `$1,000,000` over a trailing 12-month period.

## Hard Boundaries

- Do not call this legal advice.
- Do not publish final legal/creator-policy terms without Bob review and legal/accounting review.
- Do not implement Stripe products, marketplace payments, payout flows, tax reporting, or enforcement without a separate implementation brief.
- Do not promise Kickstarter backers equity, financial returns, loans, passive income, or shares of future creator-platform revenue.
